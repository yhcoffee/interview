New candidate interview

30 minutes · Consistent questions · Practical evaluation
30 MIN
Time plan: Introduction 3m · Barista experience 4m · Goals & scheduling 6m · On-bar assessment 12m · Compensation 5m

01 · Introduction & communication

3 MIN
Tell me a little about yourself outside of coffee.
What interested you in joining Yihui?

Observe natural conversation, warmth, and willingness to initiate guest interactions—not just how talkative they are.

02 · Being a barista

4 MIN
What do you like most about being a barista?
What did you dislike about your previous barista jobs?

03 · Goals & commitment

6 MIN
What are you hoping to get out of your next job, and how does it fit into what you want to do over the next year or two?
Are you hoping to grow further in coffee and hospitality, or is this something you're doing alongside other goals?
What would make a workplace somewhere you'd want to stay long-term?
What does your ideal weekly schedule look like over the next six months?

04 · On-bar practical

12 MIN

One espresso and one milk drink, followed by a station reset. Use test-only drinks; compensate any productive work.

05 · Compensation & questions

5 MIN
Our starting rate is $___ per hour plus tips. Does that align with your expectations?
What questions do you have about the position or how we operate?
